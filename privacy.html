Privacy policy: Deadline Event Sync

Deadline Event Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner (jc12055@nyu.edu).

It reads the owner's own Gmail event emails and adds events to the owner's own Google Calendar. Data stays on the owner's computer and in their Google account. It is not sold, shared, or sent to third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
